How to use it:
  1. extract parpar.exe (with 7-Zip) in the same folder than ngPost.exe
     (or in a "parpar" sub-folder)
  2. restart ngPost: it uses parpar.exe automatically instead of par2.exe
     (PAR2_PATH / PAR2_ARGS in ngPost.conf still take precedence)
